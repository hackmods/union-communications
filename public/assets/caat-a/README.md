Brand tokens from the art: primary `#B22E2C` (stacked-plate field),
accent `#003DA5` (OPSEU blue coalition tie-in). Coalition Look uses white
accent so canvas type stays knockout-white.

SVG files are path traces of the official rasters (no embedded PNG) until
faculty EPS lands. Reverse and coalition are all-white knockouts on their
plate rects. Colour and one-colour fills stay `#B22E2C`. Plated files share a
tight ~2:1 artboard with a full-bleed plate rect. Replace with vector EPS
exports when your local receives updated faculty art — keep viewBoxes tight
so Look cards stay aligned.
