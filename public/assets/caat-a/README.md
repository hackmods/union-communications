Brand tokens from the art: primary `#7B1E3F` (burgundy field),
accent `#003DA5` (OPSEU blue coalition tie-in).

SVG files are vector traces of the official lockups so Look cards stay sharp.
Reverse and coalition are all-white knockouts on their plate rects — leftover
burgundy in the OPSEU wordmark is not reverse art.
Colour lockup is a tight horizontal strip; plated files share a `200×100`
artboard with a full-bleed plate rect; one-colour stays a Brand Kit letterhead
crop (same burgundy ink as colour).
Keep viewBoxes tight so Look cards stay aligned.
