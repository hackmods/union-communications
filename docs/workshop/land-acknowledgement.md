# Land acknowledgement workshop — facilitator notes

Public outline (project this): `/guide/workshops/land-acknowledgement`

Canonical how-to + Niagara annotated example + federation sources: `/guide/land-acknowledgement`

PDF worksheet (same download on both pages): one-page walkthrough handout.

## Format

- **Duration:** ~60–90 minutes
- **Audience:** Local executive, education committee, stewards who open meetings
- **Goal:** Working session — leave with a draft and a review date, not homework

## Day-of checklist

1. Print one worksheet per person (`Download worksheet` on the outline or guide).
2. Have Native-Land.ca or Whose Land ready to project; open your federation territory PDF.
3. Tables or circle; pairs for drafting — no one forced to read aloud.
4. Close with: executive review date, who reads next meeting, one concrete action.

## Do not

- Ask the only Indigenous member in the room to educate everyone or default as reader.
- Paste a script from another city.
- Treat maps as final authority — confirm spellings with Indigenous sources.
