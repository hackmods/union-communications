# Session knowledge — Comms stay free / hosted Hub cost (2026-08-19)

**Audience:** future agents + Ryan.  
**Companions:** [ADR-019](../DECISIONS.md), [`docs/VISION.md`](../VISION.md) “What stays free”, public `/manifesto`.

## Decision

Do **not** delete `/manifesto`. Narrow the promise:

| Surface | Promise |
|---|---|
| Public Comms | Stays free. On-device. No ads, no tracking, no paywall on a poster. |
| Self-host Officer Hub / Local Portal | Free software; they pay their own server (`LICENSE`). |
| UnionOps-hosted Officer Hub / Local Portal | Hosting has a cost. Ask enough to keep the service online, not a lock-in fee. |

Coffee on `/support` covers the public site only. It is not Hub hosting.

## Do not

- Restore “free (and always will be)” / “no premium tiers” as a whole-platform promise
- Invent a price or add `/pricing` without an explicit product cut
- Advertise Officer Hub in public chrome just because this copy names it
